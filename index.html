The problem

Agents don’t know who they’re acting for. Or what your company allows.

Agents now update CRMs, email customers and change records. But permission systems were built for people, not agents.

Shared agents

A company assistant or team bot runs on one broad service account. Anyone who can message it can reach everything it can, and the tool only ever sees the bot.

Personal agents

An employee’s coding or desktop agent inherits their full permissions, under settings they chose. Company rules never come into it.

Background agents

Scheduled and autonomous agents act on their own. Nobody checks each action as it happens.

Agents working together

The person who asked gets lost at every handoff. And agents that each stay within their own permissions can, together, wipe a customer list.

So companies are stuck. AI teams want to deploy agents, but security and legal can’t answer three questions:

  1. Who asked?
  2. Was it allowed?
  3. Who approved it?

How it works

One clear AI policy, checked on every action.

  1. 01

    Write policy in plain English

    Each team sets the rules it knows best, or imports the policy document it already has. Kiok turns them into precise checks and combines them into one company-wide policy anyone can read.

    “Discounts over 20% need the sales manager’s approval.”

  2. 02

    Route agents through Kiok

    Kiok connects to the agents and tools you already use, and holds the connection between them. Every request carries the person who asked, from your company login.

  3. 03

    Every action checked before it runs

    Kiok previews exactly what an action would change, then allows, blocks or pauses it for the right approver. Every decision is logged against the person who started it, even when agents hand work to each other.

Layer 1

The person’s own permissions

An agent can only do what the person who asked can do. If Marcus can’t edit Dana’s deal, neither can his agent.

Layer 2

Company-wide policy

Even when the person is allowed, company rules apply on top: discount limits, approvals, and limits across all agents at once.

Decision

  • Allowed
  • Blocked
  • Paused

Nobody approves their own request. Every outcome names the rule and the team behind it.
